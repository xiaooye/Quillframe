# Post-generation diagnostics · Never Writer context

Use this document only after candidate prose exists. These original miniature contrasts illustrate hypotheses about failure mechanisms; they are not validated labels, an imitation corpus or a blacklist of sentence forms. Readers conducting a blind comparison must not see them before judging.

## The scene lands, then the narrator explains it again

“Spare sheets are in the cupboard.” Aunt Zhou cleared one shelf. She had really agreed to let Ashu stay; she simply would not admit it aloud.

The final sentence may be exact, or it may translate the exchange and action into a relationship verdict. Ask whether it contributes a new present judgment, mistake, choice or quality of voice for the viewpoint. If removing it leaves the reader with the same meaning, it is a strong subtraction candidate. The issue is not the presence of thought; it is completing the same layer of meaning twice. When an action is genuinely ambiguous, a specific thought may be better than another bodily performance.

## Fragments do not automatically create speed or stress

“The light died. Footsteps. Close. Stopped. Outside the door.” Every stop requests emphasis; a cluster can erase the hierarchy it tries to create. Ask whether the separated material remains a complete, intelligible unit in the working language and whether omitted subjects, actions and relations are easily recovered. Panicked perception, a character's established speech or one real landing can justify a fragment. Mobile reading alone does not justify turning complete syntax into subtitles.

## What work does the comparison perform?

“Her voice was thin ice; the silence became a swollen rope that tightened around everyone in the room.” Two unrelated image systems may supply less precision than “When she finished, no one answered.” Ask whether the association belongs to the viewpoint's experience, adds a usable shape, relation or sensation, and costs something when removed. Strangeness, intensity or density is not a defect by itself; a necessary comparison can outperform direct statement.

## Did a rewrite add significance of its own?

A prior implementation may already say, “She pinned the returned ring beneath the bill.” If a later rewrite adds, “It was not an ending but the first choice she had finally made for herself,” it may turn a working landing into a generic verdict. Compare historical stages only for diagnosis of newly added thematic gloss, aphorisms, bodily annotations, ornamental images and summarizing codas. The active runtime does not create an intermediary complete draft for cleanup.

## Is the narrator writing a thesis for the scene?

“Chen did not need to guard against the rain. Rain would pass; the real trouble was that opening the warehouse would tell everyone he had been there last night.” This may belong to a particular mind, or it may be the model arranging an immediate problem into a balanced, complete, quotable proposition. Do not blacklist contrast words. Look for a cluster of the structure, for commentary that explains significance before action, and for judgments that still read like portable maxims after names and scene objects are removed. If Chen's next move is to lock a door, showing who is approaching the warehouse will often create more present pressure.

## Has every speaker been polished into the same register?

“If leaving is more appropriate, I respect your decision.” “Respect does not mean you understand.” Both lines are grammatical, but either speaker might own them. Ask what each person wants from the listener now, which facts they already share, what relationship cost they will pay, and how age, work and urgency compress or distort the wording. Testimony, formal negotiation and ceremony may properly use complete, careful speech. The failure is a whole cast that remains equally considerate, indirect and interpretive in every situation.

## Is the problem handled gracefully on the first attempt?

The protagonist proposes a solution, another person confirms it is valid, the solution works and everyone understands its significance. The procedure may be correct while the chapter produces no new pressure on choice. Check whether the other party has an interest of their own, whether the first solution costs anything, whether a response alters the next move, and whether a resource, fact, relationship or task is traceably different afterward. A mostly negative answer points to Scene or Reader Pressure, not to another pass of verbs, fragments or comparisons.

## Serial immediacy is not a roughness recipe

Direct, speakable prose about people doing things does not require topical slang, two-line paragraphs, a humiliation beat in every chapter or an exclamation-mark cutoff. Diagnose whether the reader can enter the present want quickly, follow action–response–consequence, tell who is speaking and why they phrase it this way now, and identify what truly changed by the chapter's end. Sentence length, paragraph length, verbal tics and reward counts cannot substitute for that semantic judgment.

## Narrative time and attention

A summary such as “She checked the windows, packed the bag and left” can be excellent when leaving is routine. It may miss the scene's function if the available evidence says she is deciding whether to return. A realization might dwell on leaving her spare key with the neighbor while compressing the packing. Do not diagnose mere brevity as failure; locate which meaningful experience the prose omitted.

## Confrontation

“He became stronger and defeated the guard” reports an outcome. If the chapter promises a discovery of capability, ask whether the candidate lets the reader understand what the new capability enables and how the opponent responds. A longer exchange of unnamed blows can miss the same function. Conversely, an effortless victory or a compressed fight can be the right authorized outcome; extra resistance is not automatically better.

## Relationship and emotion

“She clenched her fingers, bit her lip and looked away” is not intrinsically defective. It becomes interchangeable when the same sequence substitutes for what this person understands, wants or can now say. A direct thought such as “He had asked about the train, not whether she was staying” can carry a specific interpretation without another gesture. It is not a rule that indirect dialogue beats explicit confession.

## Mystery and information

“He noticed something strange but said nothing” can withhold even the evidence a viewpoint character has already perceived. When the reader is meant to reason alongside that viewpoint, identify the missing observation and its effect on plausible interpretations. Delayed disclosure can still work with a different authorized viewpoint or a suspense contract. More clues, a twist or revealing the solution are not universal fixes.

## Everyday and professional pleasure

A page of tool names is not necessarily an experience of competence. Ask whether the reader can follow a meaningful discrimination: why a baker leaves one tray in the oven while removing the other, for example. Detailed naming may itself reward an expert audience. Quiet routine, companionship and recovery need no invented accident, rivalry or plot reversal.

## Comedy and lightness

A joke after every line can erase the speakers' different aims; a random slang phrase can interrupt an otherwise coherent voice. Look for an intelligible expectation and a character-specific response, not simply more punchlines. A serious response, an unhurried setup or an intentionally repetitive gag may be the right choice. A failed joke cannot be diagnosed by sentence length or a missing explicit laugh.

## Worldbuilding and wonder

Listing six unfamiliar place names may leave the reader unable to imagine where a person stands. Consider whether one usable spatial relation, custom or sensory contrast would make the scale accessible. Catalogs, panoramas and exposition remain legitimate when their pattern or voice is the intended pleasure. Wonder need not become danger, a puzzle, a usable power or a commodity.

## Record the narrow finding

Cite actual candidate passages, the relevant scene/profile boundary and a plausible counterexample to your diagnosis. Record pairwise ties and both-bad outcomes, as well as single-chapter returns, rewrites and insufficient evidence, without softening them. Keep source-case interpretations, author taste and General Craft claims separate. Never infer measured retention, commercial success or universal quality from these examples.
