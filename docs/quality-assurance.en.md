# Quality Assurance

Quillframe quality is a chain of evidence and ownership, not a single literary score. The key question is always: what can be proven mechanically, what requires semantic judgment, and which mechanism owns the failure?

## Pre-independent candidate qualification

Before an expensive or consequential independent gate, `candidate_qualification.py` binds non-independent semantic self-audit and reader-engagement results to the exact candidate, combines them with continuity evidence, and—after any repair—requires objective-preservation comparison evidence.

A pass means **qualified for independent review**, not independently approved.

## FIX + PRESERVE

<img src="assets/concepts/objective-preserving-repair.en.svg" alt="FIX plus PRESERVE quality principle with target improvement and an unchanged objective envelope baseline" width="100%" />

The objective envelope is a compact, fingerprinted set of authorized must-preserve objectives. It is not derived from rejected prose. Replacing an envelope requires explicit change authority and a link to the previous fingerprint.

A repair preservation comparison distinguishes four dimensions: target outcome, objective preservation, reader value, and character/relationship energy. Insufficient evidence stays pending. Local improvement with collateral regression fails.

## Incumbent and challenger

`quality_evolution.py` keeps exact content fingerprints for the incumbent and challenger. Registered `quality.compare` owns the semantic winner judgment. The deterministic ledger verifies the exact pair, validates the objective envelope, consumes the result once, and updates the incumbent only when the semantic result selects the challenger. It never stops revision from a mechanical streak; completion is explicit.

Absolute scores alone do not decide keep/discard.

## Repair-induced regression protection

`repair_objective_regression.py` observes the specific failure mode where the repair target improves while a protected objective degrades. `regression_escape.py` separately records when a known regression escaped its expected detection stage. Neither tool invents literary judgment; both bind existing evidence to exact artifacts and stages.

## Fresh regeneration contamination boundary

Fresh regeneration can challenge the incumbent while hiding rejected prose from its writer context. It still has a comparison parent for evaluation, but no prose parent. This is enforced through Candidate Lineage rather than inferred from prose similarity.

## Exact fingerprint review binding

Every semantic review receipt names the candidate fingerprint, semantic job fingerprint, and result fingerprint. A materially changed candidate invalidates stale review evidence. The runtime fails closed when lineage or binding is absent instead of guessing ancestry.

## Acceptance evidence is not settlement authority

Candidate Lineage can bind an opaque acceptance evidence reference to one exact artifact fingerprint. It deliberately reports `authority_verified=false` and `settlement_authorized=false`. The actual authority layer must authenticate acceptance independently.

## Independent semantic integrity

<img src="assets/concepts/independent-semantic-review.en.svg" alt="Independent review requires a separate reviewer invocation and exact candidate fingerprint binding" width="100%" />

Manager self-review is not independent review. Mechanical metadata is not semantic judgment. A valid semantic rejection is a result, not infrastructure failure, and must route back to the owning repair mechanism rather than trigger reviewer shopping.
