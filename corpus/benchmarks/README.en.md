# Generic Mechanism Benchmarks

This directory stores **cross-work, project-agnostic mechanism evidence** that can calibrate Surface/Reader profiles and generate evals without injecting raw copyrighted corpus text into the Writer.

A benchmark is not a style exemplar and not Canon.

```text
source observations
→ per-work analyses
→ counterexample/profile checks
→ mechanism benchmark
→ capability/regression evals
→ profile/guidance candidate
```

## Seed registry

`mechanisms.json` preserves the first generic benchmark family migrated into NovelForge v7:

- functional micro-action;
- decision-specific interiority;
- pressure-bound exposition;
- embodied dialogue through task/object;
- historical/institutional texture through active consequences;
- task-bound exposition dialogue;
- pressure ladder to action;
- concrete forward-pull ending.

These are mechanism abstractions. No consumer-project facts or raw source passages are stored here.

## Benchmark fields

Each entry records:
- stable benchmark ID;
- mechanism and problem addressed;
- positive operational pattern;
- failure boundary;
- applicable profiles;
- linked HF/RG/eval mechanisms;
- provenance class/status;
- writer-safe guidance.

## Promotion boundary

A benchmark may influence Generic Fundamentals only after its evidence/counterexample/eval status supports the promotion. Migrated seed mechanisms remain revisable and can be split, narrowed, or deprecated by future Corpus/Learning evidence.
