## Live evidence and limits

The [six-pair protocol](../../evals/OUTLINE_CRAFT_ABLATION.en.md) completed: 6 shared scene projections, 12 Raw Draft calls and 12 Surface calls, for 30 observed CLI turns. All 30 were submitted with unique host thread identities; there were zero model failures and zero retries. The three blinded batches contain six pairs and export no arm mapping. Human reading is now the pending gate.

The explicit author request reset only the **local round** to zero. The round now contains those 30 calls. The previous 185 charged attempts, ledger bytes and independent-dispatch evidence remain preserved and hash-bound; provider/account usage was not reset. The existing 192-attempt ceiling remains in place, and no additional calls are scheduled.
