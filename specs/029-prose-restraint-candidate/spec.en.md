# Prose-restraint candidate

2026-08-28 · `SYSTEM-IMPROVE` · Version-2 candidate; default behavior and quality claims remain unchanged.

A bounded, anonymized observation from the first version-1 reading batch identified the same failure family across compared prose: narrator gloss after already legible evidence, synthetic fragment and short-sentence clusters, and comparisons that decorate rather than clarify. The reviewer subsequently declared no prior exposure to the presented samples or A/B mapping. Two pair-level observations are therefore sealed as blind-eligible evidence against the exact batch and text fingerprints. They motivate a narrow candidate correction; they do not establish a universal rule, a winning method or version-2 quality.

Current skill research supplies both support and counterexamples. Several sources favor selective explanation, character-owned cognition, natural spoken syntax, stage-separated revision and preserving established voice. Other recipes equate mobile reading with short sentences, two-to-three-line paragraphs, fixed emotional ladders, action tags or post-payoff summaries. Version 2 adopts the mechanisms and explicitly rejects those surface quotas.

## Candidate mechanism

Only the opt-in `outline_driven` foundation changes. Version 2 asks the Writer to:

- complete one layer of meaning once, while retaining direct interiority when thought changes understanding or is itself the promised experience;
- organize sentences and paragraphs by complete semantic units rather than mobile-reading quotas or manufactured long-short alternation;
- use a comparison only when it belongs to the authorized viewpoint and adds precise information;
- let a concrete ending stand without an abstract restatement, while preserving genuine reflective endings;
- make Surface subtractive first: remove redundant gloss and ornament, recombine falsely split units, preserve voice, and add no theme, aphorism or summarizing coda.

These are semantic choices, not punctuation bans, word lists, sentence-length metrics or deterministic prose scores. Post-generation diagnostics record clustered evidence and plausible counterexamples. They never enter Writer context.

## Version, isolation and rollback

The craft registry advances from 1 to 2 and only the foundation card advances to version 2. The exact version-1 registry and bilingual foundation remain under `surface/craft/history/v1/`; existing experiment artifacts also retain their frozen version-1 snapshots. Resume continues to use the snapshot bound to its execution. Historical files are evidence, not selectable runtime authority.

New DRAFT runs still default to `baseline`. Version 2 reaches only new runs that explicitly choose `craft_guidance_mode=outline_driven`. Rollback is a new `baseline` run; no historical run, reading artifact or call ledger is rewritten.

## Evidence boundary

Deterministic tests can establish resource identity, history integrity, packaging and Writer/Reader isolation. They cannot establish better Chinese fiction. Version-1 samples cannot prove version-2 quality. A later version-2 capability/regression comparison must use a fresh frozen snapshot and reading material not previously exposed to that reviewer. No additional model calls are authorized or scheduled by this change.
