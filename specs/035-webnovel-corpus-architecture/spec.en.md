# Web-Novel Corpus Analysis Architecture v2

2026-08-31 · `SYSTEM-IMPROVE` · clean-break successor shaped by the story-analysis workflow.

Corpus v2 no longer reduces fiction to ten flat prose-style axes. It organizes evidence into six web-novel domains and learns falsifiable mechanisms at leaf dimensions: reader contract, plot progression, emotion and payoff, character and relationship, scene delivery, and language rhythm. Research remains candidate evidence, never Canon, author preference, or Framework authority.

## 01 · Objective

The system must explain which serial-fiction function a technique serves, the causal or emotional chain through which it works, and where it fails. It must not answer “whom should this imitate,” and deterministic metrics must not substitute for literary judgment.

The intended artifact is a small catalog of concise Chinese mechanism cards. Each card binds a domain and dimension to chapter position, narrative span, applicability, failure boundaries, cross-work support, and counterevidence. Writer receives only a few source-free cards relevant to the current scene.

## 02 · Six-domain hierarchy

| Domain | Leaf dimensions |
|---|---|
| Reader contract | opening hook, reader promise, chapter forward pull |
| Plot progression | causal progression, information progression, conflict escalation, setup and payoff |
| Emotion and payoff | reader need, emotional engine, setup-release-aftermath cycle, cross-chapter emotional rhythm |
| Character and relationship | character agency, character voice, relationship movement, opposition pressure |
| Scene delivery | scene entry and transition, dialogue subtext, action and reaction, interiority and viewpoint, exposition integration |
| Language rhythm | sentence rhythm, punctuation pattern, paragraph rhythm, lexical register, descriptive focus, imagery and situated humor |

Domains organize research; leaf dimensions are the unit of observation, convergence, holdout verification, and candidate compilation. Scheduling must not mechanically execute every work against every dimension. Models activate dimensions from evidence gaps; deterministic runtime enforces only bounds, budgets, identities, and bindings.

## 03 · Evidence coordinates

Every observation carries chapter position, narrative span, and a retrieval hint. Chapter positions cover the golden opening chapters, early, middle, late, and resolution. Narrative spans cover paragraph, scene, chapter, cross-chapter, serial loop, and whole-work. Existing bounded retrieval hints remain opening, dialogue, action, interiority, exposition, environment, body appearance, relationship, transition, and ending.

A retrieval hint is not a literary finding. The model classifies the actual dimension, scene function, emotion stage, and evidence gap. Deterministic code must not infer payoff, voice, relationship movement, or literary quality from keywords.

## 04 · Mechanism record

Observations and cross-work candidates use closed v2 schemas. Core fields are `domain`, `dimension`, a Chinese mechanism, reader effect, causal chain, applicability, avoid conditions, failure boundary, chapter positions, narrative spans, content zone, opaque evidence references, support summary, counterexample summary, and confidence.

A compilable candidate requires support from at least two distinct works and at least one counterexample. Counts describe coverage but do not prove the mechanism. Semantic deduplication, contradiction handling, dimension activation, and convergence belong to the model.

## 05 · Writer projection

Compilation erases works, passages, and source identity. Runtime semantic selection chooses one to four cards relevant to the current chapter task; it does not fill domain quotas or send the whole corpus, research explanations, raw anchors, or leakage reports to Writer.

Cards affect only the current realization. They cannot write Canon, mutate preference, or promote Framework behavior. Blind Readers and independent reviewers cannot see treatment identity or Corpus cards.

## 06 · Version and invalidation

The successor protocol is `quillframe_corpus_webnovel_learning_v2`. The v1 ten-axis protocol, completed V5 run, 135 old candidates, fingerprints, and blind-review evidence remain historical material. They cannot resume under v2, be relabeled as v2, or support v2 promotion.

Old schemas fail closed at the current v2 entry. There is no adapter, dual read/write, automatic migration, alias, or fallback. Rollback restores the registry default and deactivates v2 artifacts rather than reopening old inputs.

## 07 · Quality gates

Publication requires cross-work support and counterevidence, explicit applicability boundaries, held-out work replication, leave-one-work-out regression, independent semantic leakage review, frozen-input three-arm blind evaluation, order swapping, exact artifact bindings, and manual promotion approval.

Evaluation tasks must cover golden-opening hooks and chapter pull, emotional setup-release-aftermath, character voice and relationship movement, information setup and payoff, scene causality, and natural Chinese sentence/paragraph rhythm. Green engineering tests prove contract boundaries, not literary uplift.

## 08 · Story-analysis material not adopted

Raw anchors belong in a private human analysis report, not a Corpus public artifact or Writer payload. Characters, plot, setting, titles, creators, paths, quotes, close paraphrases, and source-reconstructable vectors are forbidden. Platform and genre differences may define applicability boundaries but never named-author imitation instructions.
